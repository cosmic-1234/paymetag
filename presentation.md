## Slide 2: The Problem: The $150B Cross-Border Blindspot

### **The Reality of Managing Indian Wealth from Abroad**

| Pain Point | Current Reality | Impact on NRIs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fragmented Assets** | 6–8 disparate logins across legacy banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis), depositories (CDSL/NSDL), and municipal land records. | No unified view of true net worth; missed interest or dividend payouts. |
| **Tax Ambiguity & Double Taxation** | Complex DTAA treaties, Indian TDS withholding (up to 30%), and US IRS foreign account reporting (FBAR / FATCA Form 8938). | NRIs routinely overpay advance tax or face steep IRS compliance penalties ($10,000+). |
| **Asset Freezes & KYC Delays** | Strict periodic bank KYC renewals and un-redesignated resident accounts lead to frozen international debits. | Over ₹3–5 Lakhs trapped per NRI family in dormant accounts or unlinked bank accounts. |
| **Inheritance & Land Disputes** | Opaque land title registers (7/12 extracts, mutation notices) and absence of an Indian Will. | 2–3 year probate court battles in India; property boundary disputes. |
| **Lost & Unclaimed Capital** | Dividends and old PF balances transferred to government custody (IEPF / EPFO). | Millions of dollars in forgotten shares sitting idle with complex recovery processes. |

5. **PF & Retirement Savings:** Old employer Provident Fund (EPFO) tracking, National Pension System (NPS), and tax-free 15-year PPF accounts.
6. **Insurance Policies:** Family health floaters and LIC life cover policies with automated premium reminder alerts.
7. **Taxes & US Filing (FBAR):** Form 26AS matching, 15% special treaty rate lock-in, and instant one-click pre-filled US FBAR spreadsheets.
8. **Identity & Central Verification:** Central KYC (CKYC) registry check with direct redirect to Complete KYC for restricted accounts.
9. **Will, Caretaker & Succession:** Registered Power of Attorney (POA) repository, nominee coverage audit, and Indian Will drafting.

```
 [1. Country] ──► [2. PAN Check] ──► [3. Verify Phone] ──► [4. Find Assets] ──► [5. Dashboard Ready]
```

- **Step 1: Country of Residence**

- **Step 2: PAN Verification**
  - User enters their 10-digit PAN number.

  - 6-digit confirmation code sent to linked phone.

  - Seamlessly searches and links all financial nodes (banks, stocks, property, lost dividends).
  - Shows real-time aggregated wealth (e.g., ₹1.84 Cr across 11 accounts).

  - Displays a clean summary card and immediately unlocks the full DeshBoard experience.
